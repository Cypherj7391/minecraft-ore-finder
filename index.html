For diamonds in modern Overworld versions, try mining deep underground around Y = -58 or -59, while watching for lava. Ore generation varies by version and biome.

What works in-game?

A normal GitHub Pages website cannot read your live Minecraft world or reveal hidden blocks. That needs a client-side mod, a server-side plugin with permission, or a supported world file.

Recommended setup

  1. Use a Fabric mod for the Minecraft version you play.
  2. Build a legitimate coordinate and mining helper first.
  3. Only add world scanning for single-player worlds or servers where you have permission.
